### Policy

My adamant belief, rooted in democratic principles, is that we need to expand the electorate by making it easier to vote. It's very important to me that citizens realize how valuable and important their votes and voices are and I want to do all that I can to encourage everyone to engage in the democratic process, especially at the local level. The voters' voices must be heard and respected.

​

As Chair of the Public Works Committee, I ensure that DuPage County adheres to EPA and Clean Water Act compliance and has functional treatment facilities. This, along with my work on the Economic Development, Finance, Intergovernmental, Judicial and Public Safety, and Technology Committees, is rooted in fiscal responsibility and ensuring that the residents of DuPage County have access to the resources and services they need in order to thrive.
### Experience
I am Michael L. Childress, MBA, MHRM, Vice Chair of the DuPage County Board and the first African American member elected to represent DuPage County. A resident of Bloomingdale for over 40 years, I have dedicated my life to community advocacy and public service. I serve as Chair of the Public Works Committee, where I focus on infrastructure and compliance with EPA and Clean Water Act standards. I also sit on the Finance, Judicial and Public Safety, Economic Development, Legislative, Technology, and Intergovernmental Committees.

​

Previously, I served as a Bloomingdale Park District Commissioner and was the longest-serving President of the DuPage County NAACP. As a certified domestic violence advocate, I serve on the Board of Directors for the Sisters Against Abuse Society and work with Dominion Family Services. Additionally, I am the President of Hope Fair Housing and sit on the DuPage County Historical Museum Board. Throughout my career, I have secured tens of thousands of dollars in grants and hold three bachelor's degrees and two master's degrees.
